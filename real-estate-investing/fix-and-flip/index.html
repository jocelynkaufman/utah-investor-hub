Short Projects, Taxable Gains

Fix & Flip in Utah

Buy distressed, renovate fast, sell to a retail buyer. Short holding period, bigger projects, no tenant to manage — but ordinary-income tax treatment and no rental income during the hold.

The Strategy

What Fix & Flip in Utah Means

Fix and flip means buying a property that needs work, renovating it to retail condition, and selling it to an owner-occupant or investor buyer, usually within 6–18 months.

Unlike BRRRR, there's no refinance at the end. You exit via sale and take profit as capital gain (or more often, as ordinary income if flipping is a regular activity — the IRS considers frequent flippers to be dealers).

The whole game is in the purchase price. Experienced flippers live by a formula: ARV (after-repair value) × 70% − rehab cost = max offer. Anything higher eats the profit.

The Utah Edge

Why It Works in Utah

Utah still has inventory that fits the flipping model: older housing stock in Ogden, West Valley, Clearfield, Magna, Taylorsville, Midvale, and parts of SLC proper. These areas have livable but dated homes trading below renovated comps.

Buyer demand remains strong for well-renovated homes in the $350k–$600k range. A clean, finished product usually sells within 2–4 weeks of listing.

Hard money and private lending are widely available from Utah-based sources. Loan-to-cost ratios of 80–90% are routine for experienced flippers.

Getting Started

How to Begin

1

Source below-market properties

MLS deal alerts, wholesalers, driving-for-dollars, direct mail, auctions. The deal pipeline is the business.

2

Underwrite conservatively

ARV × 70% − rehab = max offer. Add holding, closing, and selling costs. Build in a contingency.

3

Close fast with hard money

Hard money or private lending covers purchase + rehab. 3–6 month term is typical.

4

Execute the rehab on schedule

Every extra month on hold costs interest and property carrying costs. Keep the scope tight and the contractor accountable.

5

List and sell

Professional staging and photography, price at the market, be ready to negotiate. Set a target DOM and stick to it.
