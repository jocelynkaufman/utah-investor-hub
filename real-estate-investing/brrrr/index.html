Recycle Your Capital

The BRRRR Method in Utah

Buy, Rehab, Rent, Refinance, Repeat. The strategy experienced investors use to recycle the same pool of capital across multiple deals. Forced appreciation does the heavy lifting.

The Strategy

What BRRRR Method in Utah Means

BRRRR stands for Buy, Rehab, Rent, Refinance, Repeat. You buy a distressed property below market, renovate it to force appreciation (not wait for the market), rent it out at a now-higher rent, refinance against the new higher appraisal to pull your capital back out, and use that capital for the next deal.

Done right, you walk away with a cash-flowing rental and most of your original capital back — which you can redeploy into the next property. That capital-recycling is how portfolios get built.

The hard part is step 1: finding the distressed property at the right price. Sourcing matters more than any other skill in BRRRR.

The Utah Edge

Why It Works in Utah

Utah still has pockets of undervalued inventory in Ogden, West Valley, Clearfield, Magna, Taylorsville, Midvale, and older sections of Salt Lake City — older housing stock, some of it tired, priced below renovated comps.

Strong rent growth along the Wasatch Front supports the "rent" leg of BRRRR. A well-renovated rental in a good school district rents quickly.

Lender comfort with Utah investor loans makes the refi leg routine. Most Utah lenders will do a cash-out refinance after a 6–12 month seasoning period.

Getting Started

How to Begin

1

Find a distressed property below market

MLS, wholesalers, driving-for-dollars, direct mail. The deal has to make sense on paper BEFORE you commit.

2

Line up your capital + contractor

Hard money or private lending for the purchase + rehab, then a long-term refi lender for the exit. Nail down your contractor and scope before closing.

3

Execute the rehab on budget

Scope creep kills BRRRR. Stick to high-ROI improvements that move the appraisal (kitchen, bath, flooring, curb appeal) and defer the vanity projects.

4

Lease at market rent

Screen carefully and price at the new market. Rent is the engine of the refi — vacant properties don't appraise as high.

5

Refi after seasoning

Most Utah lenders want 6–12 months of ownership before a cash-out refi at the new appraised value. Pull your capital back and repeat.
