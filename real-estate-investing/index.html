What You Can Buy

Rental Property Types in Utah

Five shapes of rental property, one underlying playbook. The right shape depends on your budget, financing, and management style.

🏡

Single Family

Detached homes, the classic Utah rental. Easiest to finance, easiest to sell, attracts stable long-term tenants.

Best for: first investment

🏘️

Townhomes

Lower entry price than SFH, HOA covers exteriors. Strong rental demand from young professionals and small families.

Best for: hands-off owners

🏢

Condos

Lowest barrier to entry in many Utah markets. HOA covers exterior, but rental restrictions and fees need review.

Best for: starter near downtown SLC

🏬

Multi-family (2–4)

Duplex, triplex, fourplex. Still qualifies for residential financing (FHA, conventional). The best house-hack vehicle.

Best for: house hackers

🏤

Commercial (5+)

Apartment buildings of 5 or more units. Commercial financing, valued on income. Bigger checks and bigger upside.

Best for: experienced scalers
