Top Property Management Software for Self-Managing

Property Management

Top Property Management Software for Self-Managing can be a game-changer, simplifying tasks like rent collection, maintenance tracking, and tenant communication. Software solutions like Innago allow landlords to stay organized, save time, and offer tenants a more professional experience. Here’s an overview of Innago and other top property management tools, tailored to help Utah landlords streamline their operations and effectively manage their rentals.

Why Property Management Software Matters for Self-Managing Landlords

Self-managing landlords face the challenge of balancing property operations, tenant relations, and finances on their own. Property management software provides tools to automate and centralize these tasks, making it easier to handle day-to-day operations. Key benefits include:

  • Streamlined Rent Collection: Online payment systems reduce the chances of late payments and simplify tracking.
  • Tenant Screening and Applications: Many platforms offer online applications and tenant screening to speed up the leasing process.
  • Maintenance Management: Tenants can submit requests through the platform, keeping communication organized and responsive.


Innago: A Popular Choice for Self-Managing Landlords

Innago has gained popularity among self-managing landlords for its user-friendly interface, affordability, and essential property management features. Designed with smaller landlords in mind, Innago is free for landlords, with tenants covering payment processing fees.

  • Key Features:
    • Online Rent Collection: Tenants can set up automatic payments or one-time transactions, and landlords receive notifications for completed payments.
    • Tenant Screening: Innago offers background checks, credit reports, and eviction history screenings.
    • Maintenance Management: Tenants submit maintenance requests through the portal, allowing landlords to track and document issues easily.
    • Lease Management: Customizable lease templates make it easy to send, sign, and store digital leases.
  • Pros: Free for landlords, customizable features, easy for beginners, excellent customer support.
  • Cons: Limited advanced features for larger portfolios, less robust analytics than some competitors.

Other Top Property Management Software for Self-Managing Landlords

While Innago is a popular choice, other property management software platforms also offer valuable tools and features for landlords who manage their own properties. Here are some alternatives worth considering:

1. Buildium

Buildium is a comprehensive property management platform suitable for landlords managing multiple properties. It offers extensive accounting features, tenant screening, and reporting options that make it ideal for growing portfolios.

  • Key Features:
    • Advanced accounting, including automatic payment tracking and expense categorization
    • Tenant screening with background and credit checks
    • Online applications and customizable lease templates
    • Maintenance tracking and vendor management
  • Pros: Robust accounting tools, detailed reporting, excellent scalability for larger portfolios.
  • Cons: Higher monthly fees, which start at around $50, may be cost-prohibitive for single-property landlords.

2. Avail

Avail is another platform tailored to self-managing landlords with smaller portfolios. Its interface is easy to navigate, and it includes features like tenant screening, online applications, and maintenance tracking.

  • Key Features:
    • Online rent collection with automated reminders
    • Tenant screening with credit, background, and eviction checks
    • Lease creation and e-signatures
    • Maintenance request management
  • Pros: Free basic plan with affordable premium options, ideal for landlords with a few properties.
  • Cons: Limited advanced features, particularly in reporting and analytics.

3. TurboTenant

TurboTenant is known for its simple and intuitive design, making it easy for first-time landlords to get started. It’s free for landlords, with tenants covering application and payment processing fees.

  • Key Features:
    • Free online rental applications and screening
    • Customizable lease agreements and e-signatures
    • Basic maintenance tracking and reminders
    • Automatic listing syndication to rental sites like Zillow and Apartments.com
  • Pros: Free for landlords, easy to use, great for marketing vacant properties.
  • Cons: Limited support options, lacks advanced accounting and reporting features.

4. Cozy (now part of Apartments.com)

Cozy, which has now merged with Apartments.com, offers essential tools for property management at no cost to landlords. It’s popular among landlords who want basic features without a monthly fee.

  • Key Features:
    • Online rent collection with ACH transfers
    • Tenant screening with credit and background checks
    • Maintenance request submission and tracking
    • Automatic listing syndication to Apartments.com
  • Pros: Free for landlords, integrates with Apartments.com for easier listing and syndication.
  • Cons: Limited customer support, lacks advanced lease customization options.

5. TenantCloud

TenantCloud is a flexible property management tool that includes essential features as well as additional options for landlords managing multiple properties. It offers both free and paid tiers, making it scalable as your portfolio grows.

  • Key Features:
    • Online rent collection, lease management, and tenant screening
    • Customizable accounting tools with income and expense tracking
    • Maintenance request management with photo and document storage
    • Integration with QuickBooks for seamless financial management
  • Pros: Scalable, useful for landlords with several properties, integrates well with financial software.
  • Cons: Free version has limited features; advanced features are only available in paid tiers starting around $12/month.

Choosing the Best Software for Your Rental Management Needs

When selecting property management software, consider these factors to find the best fit for your specific needs:

  1. Number of Properties: For single-property landlords, simple platforms like Innago, Cozy, or TurboTenant may be sufficient. Landlords with larger portfolios may prefer more comprehensive software like Buildium or TenantCloud.
  2. Budget: While some platforms are free, others have monthly fees that can add up. Weigh the cost against the features you need; free platforms often cover basics, but paid options offer advanced tools.
  3. Desired Features: Identify your must-have features. If you need strong accounting tools, Buildium or TenantCloud may be ideal. For straightforward rent collection and tenant screening, Innago or Avail may be enough.
  4. Scalability: If you plan to grow your portfolio, choose software that can support your expansion, such as Buildium or TenantCloud, which both offer more advanced features as your needs evolve.

Final Thoughts

Property management software helps self-managing landlords streamline tasks, improve tenant relations, and maximize efficiency. Innago is an excellent choice for Utah landlords seeking a free, easy-to-use platform, while alternatives like Buildium, Avail, and TenantCloud offer additional features for more complex needs. With the right software, managing your rental property can be simpler, more organized, and ultimately more profitable.
